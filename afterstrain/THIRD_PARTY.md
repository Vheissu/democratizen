# Third-party materials

Country outlines are from Natural Earth, distributed through world-atlas 2.0.2. Natural Earth data is public domain. Country labels and boundaries are the dataset's cartographic representation, not a political endorsement. This low-resolution map omits some small territories.

- Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- world-atlas (ISC): https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
- topojson-client (ISC): https://github.com/topojson/topojson-client
- Capacitor and official plugins (MIT): https://github.com/ionic-team/capacitor
- Vite (MIT): https://github.com/vitejs/vite

Dependency versions are pinned in `package-lock.json`. The game uses system fonts. No artwork, names, copy, or audio from Plague Inc is included.
